Research interests

01
Current focus

Dance, well-being & postgraduate life

How can creative dance help postgraduate students reconnect with their bodies and navigate the pressures of academic life? I explore experiences of stress, self-awareness and everyday well-being that standardized measures may not fully capture.

Current project · Loosening the Academic Body
02

Creativity & mental health

Evidence synthesis on creative activities, psychological well-being and how both are defined and measured.

03

Dance education & research quality

Creative thinking, curricula and stronger conceptual and methodological foundations for dance research.

04

Technology-enhanced dance learning

Digital media and AI in dance education, with artistic and embodied learning kept in view.
